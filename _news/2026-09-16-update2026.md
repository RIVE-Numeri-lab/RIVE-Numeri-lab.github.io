# 2026-2027 Update

Following our annual meeting, the Numérilab has now formed its student committee for 2026-2027.

This year, it will see the return of Jade Dormoy-Boulanger and Thierry Laurent-St-Pierre, 
as well as the arrival of Alexandre Laborie, Françoise Grenier and Jawad Ziyad.

Our calendar has also been updated with the first three events of the season, starting on October 8th. 

The remainder of the calendar, for 2027, will be announced later this fall.

See you soon.
